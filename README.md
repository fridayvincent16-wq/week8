AnalystLab Africa – Week 8 Data Analytics

HealthConnect Patient Appointment Analysis

Overview

Week 8 continues the HealthConnect patient appointment analysis from Week 7, with a stronger focus on validating the dashboard findings, identifying key patterns, and translating the analysis into actionable business insights.

The project uses the Data Analytics track to examine patient appointment attendance, no-shows, cancellations, waiting time, and factors associated with missed appointments


Project Objectives

The objectives of this week’s analysis were to:

* Validate the key findings from the previous week’s dashboard.
* Analyse patient attendance and no-show patterns.
* Identify factors associated with higher no-show rates.
* Examine waiting time and appointment lead time.
* Identify patient segments that may require attention.
* Translate analytical findings into practical recommendations.
* Present the findings in a clear and decision-oriented format.

⸻

Key KPIs

The analysis tracks the following key performance indicators:

* Total Patients: 1,696
* No-Show Rate: 48.46%
* Attendance Rate: 46.28%
* Cancellation Rate: 5.26%
* Average Waiting Time: 24.19 minutes

These KPIs provide an overview of patient appointment behaviour and operational performance.

⸻

Key Findings

The analysis identified several important patterns:

Appointment Lead Time

Patients with 31 or more days between scheduling and the appointment recorded a no-show rate of 60.49%.

This suggests that longer waiting periods between booking and appointment dates may be associated with a higher likelihood of missed appointments.

Previous No-Shows

Patients with 3 or more previous no-shows recorded a no-show rate of 68.82%.

This indicates that previous appointment behaviour is an important factor when analysing future attendance patterns.

Combined High-Risk Segment

The combined segment identified through the analysis recorded a no-show rate of 67.88%.

This highlights the importance of examining multiple patient characteristics together rather than relying on a single factor.

Distance

Patients travelling 21 km or more recorded a no-show rate of 57.76%.

Distance may therefore be an additional factor worth considering when reviewing appointment attendance patterns.

⸻

Business Insights

The analysis suggests that missed appointments are not evenly distributed across all patients.

Key areas requiring attention include:

* Patients with long appointment lead times.
* Patients with repeated previous no-shows.
* Patients within identified high-risk segments.
* Patients travelling longer distances to appointments.

These patterns can help healthcare organisations focus appointment-management efforts where they may have the greatest operational relevance.

⸻

Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. Strengthen appointment reminders for patients with long lead times.
2. Monitor patients with repeated previous no-shows and consider targeted follow-up.
3. Use patient segmentation to identify groups requiring additional appointment support.
4. Consider distance-related barriers when designing appointment reminder and engagement strategies.
5. Continue monitoring waiting time to identify opportunities for improving the patient experience.
6. Track these KPIs regularly to measure changes in appointment attendance behaviour.

Limitations

The analysis has some limitations:

* The analysis identifies relationships and patterns but does not establish causation.
* Some patient behaviour may be influenced by factors not captured in the available dataset.
* Distance and lead-time patterns should be interpreted as analytical associations rather than proof of direct causation.
